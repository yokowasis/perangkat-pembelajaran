## **C. SPEAKING**

**Target**: Present a Video Tutorial / Illustrated Manual project fluently to the class and participate in peer evaluation with compassionate communication.

### **Speaking Activity 1: Tutorial Presentation Pitch**

Present your group's Video Tutorial or Illustrated Manual project in front of the class (3-5 minutes). Follow this presentation structure:

1. **Greeting & Hook**: *"Assalamu'alaikum and good morning everyone! Welcome to our tutorial..."*
2. **Goal & KBC Connection**: *"Today we will show you how to... This project reflects Cinta Ilmu / Cinta Lingkungan because..."*
3. **Materials & Steps Pitch**: *"To start, you will need... Step 1 is to... Step 2..."*
4. **Conclusion & Call to Action**: *"We hope this tutorial inspires you to practice this at home! Thank you for watching."*

### **Speaking Activity 2: Compassionate Q&A and Peer Evaluation**

After another group presents, provide one positive appreciation and ask one thoughtful question using polite expressions:
- *"I really admire how clear your visual cues were in Step 3! How did you..."*
- *"Your explanation of water-saving tips was very inspiring! Could you clarify..."*

**Assessment Criteria**:
- **Pronunciation & Intonation**: Accurate stress on action verbs and clear vocal delivery.
- **Fluency & Engagement**: Smooth speech rate, steady eye contact, and enthusiasm.
- **KBC Synthesis**: Expressing appreciation and respect during peer feedback sessions.

## **D. READING**

**Target**: Read authentic text on creating educational video tutorials, analyze main idea, structural organization, details, inferences, and reflect on *Cinta Ilmu*.

### **Reading Text**

> ### **Creating Impactful Educational Tutorials: A Step-by-Step Guide for Young Creators**
>
> In the digital era, young people are no longer just passive consumers of content; they have become active creators of knowledge. Creating step-by-step video tutorials or illustrated manuals is a powerful way to practice *Cinta Ilmu* (Love for Knowledge) by sharing useful skills with peers around the world. However, producing a successful tutorial requires careful planning and structured execution.
>
> **Goal**: To design and film a clear, engaging, and high-impact educational tutorial.
>
> **Materials / Tools**: A smartphone camera, tripod, lapel microphone, video editing software (e.g., CapCut or Canva), script template, and topic materials.
>
> **Essential Steps**:
> 1. **Plan with a Purpose**: Choose a topic that solves a real problem in your school or community, such as *How to Compost Organic Waste*, *How to Organize Study Schedules Mindfully*, or *How to Practice Safe Cyber Hygiene*. Ensure your goal aligns with positive moral and environmental values.
> 2. **Script Audio and Visuals Parallelly**: Divide your script into two columns: *Visual Framing* (showing what the camera sees) and *Spoken Narration* (writing exact imperative sentences and sequence connectors). This prevents awkward pauses during filming.
> 3. **Record with Professional Adab**: Find a quiet, well-lit space. Speak clearly at a moderate pace, maintaining warm eye contact with the lens. Demonstrate each action step slowly so viewers can replicate your movement easily.
> 4. **Edit for Clarity and Accessibility**: Trim unnecessary footage. Add bold text captions for key imperative action verbs (*Wash, Connect, Mix, Filter*) and embed background music at a low volume so it does not drown out your voice.
> 5. **Include a Value Reflection & Call to Action**: End your tutorial not just with a summary of steps, but with a brief reflection on how this skill promotes *Panca Cinta*—whether by saving energy, helping classmates, or honoring knowledge.
>
> When youth combine technical communication skills with a sincere heart to serve others, a simple procedure text becomes a catalyst for positive social change.

### **Comprehension Questions**

1. **Main Idea**: What is the main message of the reading text regarding young content creators today?
2. **Detailed Information**: What are the two columns recommended in Step 2, and why is this parallel structure important?
3. **Inference**: Why does adding bold text captions for key imperative verbs improve the educational impact of a video tutorial?
4. **Vocabulary & Reference**: Find words in the text that mean:
   - (a) *"A person who buys or uses goods and information (paragraph 1)"*
   - (b) *"An event or person that causes important change to happen (paragraph 6)"*
5. **KBC Reflection**: How does sharing educational tutorials online fulfill the prophetic teaching of sharing beneficial knowledge (*ilmun yuntafa'u bih*)?

**Assessment**: Students must correctly answer at least 4 out of 5 comprehension questions with thorough, text-supported responses.
