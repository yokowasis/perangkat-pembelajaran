## D. READING

Read the text below carefully and answer the comprehension questions.

### Reading Text: Sultan Muhammad Salahuddin — The Patriot of Bima

Sultan Muhammad Salahuddin was one of the most prominent leaders in the history of Bima, West Nusa Tenggara. He was born in Bima and served as the last reigning Sultan of Bima from 1915 until his death in 1951. Throughout his life, he dedicated his efforts to Islamic education, social welfare, and national independence.

During his early reign in the 1920s, Sultan Salahuddin realized that education was the key to empowering his people. He established modern Islamic schools (*madrasah*) across Bima, encouraging both boys and girls to seek knowledge. He believed that seeking knowledge (*Cinta Ilmu*) was a religious obligation that would liberate his people from ignorance and poverty.

When Indonesia proclaimed its independence on August 17, 1945, Sultan Salahuddin demonstrated extraordinary patriotism (*Cinta Tanah Air*). Without hesitation, he officially declared that the Sultanate of Bima was an integral part of the newly born Republic of Indonesia. He mobilised local youth, provided financial support for freedom fighters, and refused to cooperate with returning colonial forces.

In his final years, Sultan Salahuddin remained a humble and compassionate leader. He frequently visited rural villages in Bima to ensure that no citizen suffered from hunger. His life story teaches us that true leadership is rooted in love for Allah, love for knowledge, and love for one's homeland (*Hubbul Wathan minal Iman*).

### Reading Comprehension Questions

1. Who was Sultan Muhammad Salahuddin, and what was his historical role in Bima?
2. What major step did Sultan Salahuddin take in the 1920s to empower the people of Bima?
3. How did Sultan Salahuddin respond when Indonesia proclaimed its independence in 1945?
4. What actions did he take to support the Indonesian freedom fighters?
5. Find 5 action verbs in the past tense (*simple past tense*) used in the text!
6. Identify the **Orientation**, **Events**, and **Reorientation** of the text above!
7. What value of KBC (*Cinta Tanah Air / Hubbul Wathan*) can we adopt from Sultan Salahuddin's life story as madrasah students?

**Assessment Target**: Menjawab minimal 6 dari 7 pertanyaan pemahaman dengan tepat dan mampu mengidentifikasi generic structure teks sejarah.

### Task 3: Guided Recount Writing (Personal or Historical Event)

Write a short **Recount Text** (100–120 words) about ONE of the following topics:
- **Topic A**: A memorable personal experience where you felt grateful to Allah Swt. (Cinta Diri & Sesama / Gratitude for Life).
- **Topic B**: A short historical recount about a Indonesian/Bima hero who inspires you (Cinta Tanah Air / Heroic Struggle).
