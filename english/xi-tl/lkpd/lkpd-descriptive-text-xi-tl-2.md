## 1. Vocabulary Section

**Objective:** To master sophisticated vocabulary for advanced descriptive writing, focusing on emotional resonance and environmental appreciation.

Study the following advanced adjectives:
- **Ethereal**: extremely delicate and light in a way that seems too perfect for this world (Sangat anggun/seperti bukan dari dunia ini)
- **Altruistic**: showing a disinterested and selfless concern for the well-being of others (Mengutamakan orang lain/penuh kasih)
- **Resilient**: able to withstand or recover quickly from difficult conditions (Tangguh)
- **Serene**: calm, peaceful, and untroubled (Tenang/damai)
- **Captivating**: capable of attracting and holding interest; charming (Menawan hati)

**Task**: Choose 3 of the words above. Write a paragraph describing a person who has overcome difficulties (Resilient/Altruistic) OR a natural place that has been restored (Ethereal/Serene/Captivating).
**Assessment**: Students must use at least 3 advanced words correctly within a cohesive paragraph, showing depth of meaning.

## 2. Listening Section

**Objective:** To analyze implied meanings and speakers' attitudes from an advanced descriptive audio text.

**Instructions:**
Listen to the audio (provided by the teacher) titled "The Unsung Heroes of the Forest". Answer the analytical questions below!

1. What is the speaker's main attitude towards the forest rangers?
2. How does the speaker describe the impact of deforestation on the *serene* landscape?
3. Mention two specific details the speaker uses to describe the rangers' *altruistic* nature.
4. According to the audio, how does loving nature (Cinta Lingkungan) reflect our love for the Creator?
5. Fill in the missing phrase: "Their _________ efforts have brought life back to the barren land."

**Assessment**: Students must answer at least 4 out of 5 questions correctly, demonstrating the ability to infer meaning and extract specific details.

---

## 3. Speaking Section

**Objective:** To fluently debate and present descriptive arguments promoting environmental conservation and empathy.

**Instructions:**
In groups of three, prepare a 3-minute presentation describing a severely polluted local river or a neglected public facility. 
- Use vivid, descriptive language to paint a picture of its current state.
- Then, describe your "dream" vision of how it *should* look if people showed more love and responsibility (Cinta Lingkungan & Sesama).

**Task**: Present your description to the class. Use persuasive and expressive intonation.
**Assessment**: Evaluated on advanced pronunciation, fluency, expressive intonation, and the use of sophisticated descriptive vocabulary.

## 4. Reading Section

**Objective:** To conduct a literary analysis of a complex descriptive text, focusing on imagery and author's purpose.

**Instructions:**
Read the advanced text below.

**The Resilience of Sumba**
Sumba is not merely a destination; it is an ethereal masterpiece woven by the hands of the Creator. The majestic hills roll endlessly, covered in a blanket of golden savannah that whispers in the wind. However, what truly makes Sumba captivating is its people. Despite the harsh, dry climate that often tests their endurance, the Sumbanese remain incredibly resilient. Their altruistic spirit shines through their communal traditions, where no neighbor is left behind during times of scarcity. To visit Sumba is to witness a perfect harmony between human endurance and nature's raw beauty, a testament to true love for life.

**Questions:**
1. What literary device is used in the phrase "woven by the hands of the Creator"? What does it imply?
2. How does the author contrast the physical environment with the character of the people?
3. Find an example of personification in the text and explain its effect!
4. How is the value of 'Cinta Sesama' (Love for Others) depicted in the text?
5. What is the author's primary purpose in writing this descriptive passage?

**Assessment**: Students must provide analytical answers, successfully identifying literary devices and inferring the author's intent.

## 5. Writing Section

**Objective:** To write a sophisticated, multi-paragraph descriptive essay using advanced structure, varied sentence patterns, and vivid imagery.

**Instructions:**
Write a descriptive essay (3 paragraphs) about a place that holds deep emotional significance to you, or a person who has profoundly shaped your character.
- **Paragraph 1**: Engaging introduction with a hook and clear identification.
- **Paragraph 2**: Detailed physical description using sensory details (sight, sound, smell).
- **Paragraph 3**: Emotional description explaining why this person/place deserves love and protection (integrating KBC values).

**Assessment**: Evaluated on grammatical complexity, coherence, sensory imagery, and the seamless integration of KBC values.
