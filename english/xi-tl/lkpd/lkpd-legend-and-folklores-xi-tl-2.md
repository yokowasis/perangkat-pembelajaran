### 4. Reading Section

Bacalah artikel analisis sastra berikut!

#### Text: Reimagining Nusantara Folklores Through the Lens of Love and Empathy

Traditional folklores and legends form the bedrock of Indonesia's rich oral tradition. For centuries, stories such as *Malin Kundang*, *Sangkuriang*, and *Batu Golog* have been passed down from generation to generation to teach morality and social norms. However, traditional versions of these stories often emphasize severe retribution—characters who commit errors are transformed into stones, turned into animals, or consumed by tragedy.

While these traditional endings served historical purposes of instilling fear and discipline, modern educators under Kurikulum Berbasis Cinta (KBC) suggest a transformative approach: reading and rewriting folklores through a compassionate lens. 

Critically analyzing a legend does not mean erasing its history; rather, it means exploring the deeper psychological motives of characters. Why did Malin Kundang feel ashamed? Why was the mother overwhelmed by anger? By examining these questions with empathy (*tafahum*), students learn to recognize human flaws without reducing human dignity strictly to their worst mistakes.

Furthermore, rewriting folklores with a compassionate Point of View (POV) empowers students to imagine alternative resolutions rooted in restorative justice. In a retold narrative, a character who strays is given space for self-reflection (*muhasabah*), repentance (*taubat*), and active contribution to society. 

This educational approach bridges ancient heritage with contemporary character building. It teaches students that true strength lies not in cursing or condemning, but in forgiving, healing, and nurturing *Cinta Diri & Sesama* (Love for Self and Others) and *Cinta Tanah Air* (Love for Homeland).

#### Critical Reading Comprehension Questions:
1. **Main Idea**: What is the central argument presented in the text regarding traditional Indonesian folklores?
2. **Comparative Analysis**: How do traditional endings of legends differ from the transformative approach suggested by Kurikulum Berbasis Cinta (KBC)?
3. **Inference**: According to the text, why is exploring the psychological motives of characters important for character building?
4. **Vocabulary in Context**: What does the phrase "restorative justice" mean in the context of rewriting narrative stories?
5. **KBC Character Reflection**: How can reimagining legends with a compassionate POV help you handle interpersonal conflicts in your daily school life?

**Assessment Target**: Jawaban analisis kritis yang komprehensif, tepat logika, dan mencerminkan pemahaman mendalam atas pembentukan karakter berbelas kasih (Skor minimal 80/100).

#### Activity 5.1: Rewriting a Legend with a Compassionate POV (FIDS Framework)

*Langkah Penulisan (Alur FIDS):*
- **Feel**: Gambarkan penyesalan batin tokoh dan rasa empati orang di sekitarnya.
- **Imagine**: Bayangkan dialog pemaafan dan dorongan untuk bertobat/memperbaiki diri.
- **Do**: Tuliskan tindakan nyata tokoh dalam memperbaiki kesalahan masa lalunya (misal: membangun fasilitas umum, mengabdi pada masyarakat).
- **Share / Coda**: Akhiri cerita dengan pesan moral KBC yang menyentuh hati.
