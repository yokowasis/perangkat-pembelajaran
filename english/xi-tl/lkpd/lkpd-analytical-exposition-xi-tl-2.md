## **D. READING**

**Target**: Critically evaluate an authentic analytical exposition text on local heritage preservation and youth responsibility.

### **Reading Text**

**Preserving Bima's Cultural and Natural Heritage Through Eco-Tourism**

Bima, located in West Nusa Tenggara, is rich in cultural heritage, historical sites like *Asi Mbojo* (the Bima Sultanate Palace), and breathtaking landscapes such as Mount Tambora and Wawo highlands. However, rapid modernization and uncontrolled tourism development pose significant threats to these cultural and natural assets. Young intellectuals in Bima must actively advocate for sustainable eco-tourism to preserve our ancestral heritage for future generations (*Cinta Tanah Air*).

First, eco-tourism fosters environmental conservation while honoring local cultural traditions. Unlike mass tourism, which often results in deforestation and littering, eco-tourism emphasizes minimal ecological footprint and community-led conservation. By protecting historical landmarks and natural reserves, we honor the historical legacy of our ancestors who lived in harmony with nature.

Second, sustainable tourism empowers the local economy without exploiting natural resources. When local communities host eco-friendly tours, showcase traditional *Mbojo* weaving (*Uma Lengge* and *Manta* traditions), and sell authentic local cuisine, financial benefits flow directly to families in Bima. Consequently, economic growth is achieved sustainably without destroying our ecosystem.

Finally, embracing cultural preservation strengthens youth identity and pride (*Cinta Tanah Air*). In a globalized world, knowing and defending one's roots prevents cultural alienation. When youth write articles, create digital campaigns, and participate in heritage conservation, they demonstrate true patriotism grounded in knowledge and action.

In conclusion, advocating for eco-tourism and heritage preservation in Bima is essential for environmental integrity, economic dignity, and cultural pride. It is imperative that youth, schools, and government institutions collaborate to safeguard Bima's priceless heritage.
