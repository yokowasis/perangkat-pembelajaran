## 1. Vocabulary Section

**Objective:** To learn essential action verbs and adjectives to express feelings and actions in the past, fostering gratitude for personal experiences.

Read the following words carefully. They are used to recount past events.
- **Overjoyed**: extremely happy (Sangat bahagia)
- **Exhausted**: completely tired (Sangat kelelahan)
- **Supported**: gave assistance or encouragement (Mendukung)
- **Participated**: took part in an action or endeavor (Berpartisipasi)
- **Memorable**: worth remembering or easily remembered (Berkesan)

**Task**: Write 5 sentences using the Simple Past Tense and the words above to recount a positive experience you had at school or home.
**Assessment**: Students must correctly use at least 4 out of 5 words in meaningful past tense sentences.

---

## 2. Listening Section

**Objective:** To extract specific chronological information from a personal recount audio.

**Instructions:**
Listen to the audio (provided by the teacher) about "My First School Festival". Fill in the blanks below!

1. Last month, our school held a ___________ festival.
2. First, my friends and I ___________ our food stall early in the morning.
3. We were very ___________ because many students bought our snacks.
4. After that, we ___________ in the traditional dance competition.
5. It was a very ___________ day, and I felt grateful for my friends' help.

**Assessment**: Students must answer at least 4 blanks correctly based on the listening track to demonstrate accurate information extraction.

---

## 3. Speaking Section

**Objective:** To practice fluency and pronunciation while recounting a shared past experience, emphasizing teamwork and empathy.

**Instructions:**
Work with a partner. Practice the following dialogue with correct pronunciation and intonation.

**A**: "Do you remember when we joined the camping trip last year?"
**B**: "Yes! I was totally exhausted after the hike, but it was so memorable."
**A**: "I agree. I was touched when everyone supported you when you tripped."
**B**: "That's true friendship. We participated in every game together."
**A**: "It made me realize how important it is to care for one another."

**Task**: Perform the dialogue in front of the teacher.
**Assessment**: Pronunciation clarity, fluency, and the ability to convey the emotional tone (gratitude and empathy) will be evaluated.

## 4. Reading Section

**Objective:** To comprehend the chronological order and emotional reflection of a personal recount text.

**Instructions:**
Read the text below and answer the questions.

**A Day at the Orphanage**
Last Sunday, my classmates and I visited a local orphanage. We wanted to share our happiness and spend time with the children there. First, we introduced ourselves and played some ice-breaking games. The children looked overjoyed when we handed out the toys we had collected. Then, we ate lunch together and listened to their stories. Finally, before going home, we took a group photo. Although I felt a bit exhausted, it was a truly memorable experience. It taught me to be more grateful for what I have and to always love others.

**Questions:**
1. What is the main idea of the text?
2. What did the writer and their classmates do first when they arrived?
3. How did the children feel when they received the toys?
4. Mention two action verbs (Verb 2) found in the text!
5. What moral lesson (Cinta Sesama) did the writer learn from this experience?

**Assessment**: Students must be able to identify the chronological events and infer the moral lesson accurately.

---

## 5. Writing Section

**Objective:** To produce a short personal recount text using chronological order and the Simple Past Tense.

**Instructions:**
Think of a memorable day you spent with your family or friends (e.g., a holiday, a competition, or a simple day out). Write a short recount text (3 paragraphs).
- **Paragraph 1 (Orientation)**: Who, where, and when did it happen?
- **Paragraph 2 (Events)**: Write the events in chronological order (use *first, then, after that*).
- **Paragraph 3 (Re-orientation)**: State your feelings about the experience and what you learned from it (integrating KBC values: gratitude/Cinta Diri).

**Assessment**: Grammar accuracy (Simple Past Tense), chronological coherence (Temporal connectives), and structure will be assessed.
