## 1. Vocabulary Section

**Objective:** To master advanced adjectives and reporting verbs used in complex narratives and fantasy stories.

Study the following advanced vocabulary words:
- **Enchanting**: delightfully charming or attractive (Mempesona/Memikat)
- **Malevolent**: having or showing a wish to do evil to others (Jahat/Kejam)
- **Vanquished**: defeated thoroughly (Dikalahkan)
- **Whispered**: spoke very softly using one's breath (Berbisik)
- **Declared**: said something in a solemn and emphatic manner (Menyatakan dengan tegas)

**Task**: Choose 3 of the words above. Write a short paragraph describing a climax scene in a fantasy story where the hero confronts the villain.
**Assessment**: Students must use at least 3 advanced words correctly within a cohesive past tense paragraph.

## 2. Listening Section

**Objective:** To analyze character dialogue (Direct Speech) and infer underlying motives from a fairytale audio.

**Instructions:**
Listen to the audio (provided by the teacher) titled "The True Maleficent". Answer the analytical questions below!

1. What did the King *declare* when his daughter was born?
2. Quote the exact words (Direct Speech) the dark fairy *whispered* to the baby.
3. How did the story describe the *enchanting* forest where the fairy lived?
4. Why did the fairy actually cast the spell? (Analyze her true motive).
5. Fill in the blank: "The *malevolent* curse was finally broken not by a prince, but by ___________."

**Assessment**: Students must answer at least 4 out of 5 questions correctly, demonstrating the ability to transcribe direct speech and analyze motives.

---

## 3. Speaking Section

**Objective:** To fluently debate and present an alternate perspective of a classic fairytale villain (Cinta Sesama - Empathy).

**Instructions:**
In pairs, choose a famous fairytale villain (e.g., The Evil Queen, The Big Bad Wolf). 
- Prepare a 2-minute "Defense Speech" from the villain's perspective.
- Explain *why* they acted that way (perhaps they were misunderstood or hurt in the past).
- Use persuasive intonation and direct speech.

**Task**: Present your defense speech to the class.
**Assessment**: Evaluated on advanced pronunciation, creative empathy, expressive intonation, and the use of reporting verbs.

---

## 4. Reading Section

**Objective:** To conduct a textual analysis of Direct and Indirect Speech within a narrative text.

**Instructions:**
Read the excerpt below.

**The Glass Mountain**
The brave knight stood before the towering glass mountain. He looked at the imprisoned princess at the peak.
"I will save you, no matter the cost!" the knight shouted bravely.
The old wizard, standing nearby, shook his head slowly. He warned the knight that the mountain was cursed and many had perished trying to climb it.
"I do not fear curses," declared the knight.
The wizard sighed and muttered that bravery without wisdom was just foolishness.

**Questions:**
1. Identify all sentences written in Direct Speech!
2. Identify all sentences written in Indirect Speech!
3. Change this Direct Speech into Indirect Speech: *The knight shouted bravely, "I will save you!"*
4. Change this Indirect Speech into Direct Speech: *He warned the knight that the mountain was cursed.*
5. What is the philosophical meaning behind the wizard's words: "bravery without wisdom is just foolishness"?

**Assessment**: Students must accurately identify and convert between Direct and Indirect Speech, and demonstrate critical thinking in the last question.

---

## 5. Writing Section

**Objective:** To write a sophisticated *Alternate Ending* for a classic fairytale, utilizing Direct Speech and KBC values.

**Instructions:**
Choose a well-known fairytale (e.g., Cinderella, Snow White, or Bawang Merah Bawang Putih).
Write a 3-paragraph *Alternate Ending* where the conflict is resolved without violence or punishment, but through forgiveness and understanding (Cinta Sesama).
- **Paragraph 1**: Describe the climax of the story.
- **Paragraph 2**: Write a dialogue (Direct Speech) where the protagonist confronts the antagonist with empathy, not anger.
- **Paragraph 3**: Describe the new, peaceful resolution (Coda).

**Assessment**: Evaluated on grammatical complexity (Past Tense & Direct Speech rules), creative plot twist, and the deep integration of KBC values (forgiveness).
