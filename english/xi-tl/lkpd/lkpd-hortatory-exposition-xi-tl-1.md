## **C. SPEAKING**

**Target**: Compare text structures (Analytical vs Hortatory Exposition) and express persuasive recommendations using modal auxiliaries in a pair conversation.

### **Speaking Activity 1: Analytical vs Hortatory Dialogue Practice**

Practice the following dialogue with your partner with proper intonation, clear articulation, and persuasive tone:

**Ahmad**: "Hi Nisa! Have you read this article about plastic waste management in Bima?"  
**Nisa**: "Yes, I have! The article explains why plastic waste is harmful to our rivers and oceans. But it feels like an analytical exposition because it only states facts."  
**Ahmad**: "I agree. In a hortatory exposition, the writer shouldn't just state facts; they **must recommend concrete actions**!"  
**Nisa**: "Exactly! For example, the text **should recommend** that all students bring refillable tumblers and that schools **ought to ban** single-use plastic wrappers."  
**Ahmad**: "Spot on! By taking action, we demonstrate our **Cinta Lingkungan** (Love for Environment) as a true expression of faith."  
**Nisa**: "Let's present this recommendation to our class today!"

### **Speaking Activity 2: Role-Play Recommendation**

Work in pairs. Choose one of the social or environmental issues below and create a 2-minute dialogue recommending action:

- **Option A**: Stopping online gossip and cyberbullying among teenagers (**Cinta Diri & Sesama**).
- **Option B**: Reducing plastic waste and planting trees in madrasah (**Cinta Lingkungan**).

**Speaking Guidelines & Requirements:**
- Use at least 3 modal auxiliaries (*should*, *must*, *ought to*, *had better*).
- Use at least 2 abstract nouns (*responsibility*, *awareness*, *empathy*, *sustainability*).
- Deliver your recommendation with respectful, compassionate communication.

**Assessment**: Evaluated on pronunciation, fluency, correct modal auxiliary usage, and persuasive impact.

## **D. READING**

**Target**: Analyze authentic hortatory exposition texts, identify thesis, arguments, and recommendations, and evaluate language features.

### **Reading Text**

**Why Schools Must Foster Digital Empathy and Stop Cyberbullying**

In today's digital era, social media has become an integral part of adolescent life. While it provides opportunities for connectivity and learning, it also presents significant challenges, most notably cyberbullying. Cyberbullying refers to repeated, harmful online behavior intended to intimidate, humiliate, or harass others. Because cyberbullying severely impairs student well-being, it is imperative that schools, parents, and youth take active, compassionate measures to cultivate digital empathy and eliminate online toxicity.

First and foremost, cyberbullying causes severe psychological distress and emotional isolation among victims. Unlike traditional face-to-face bullying, digital harassment can happen anytime and follow students into their private spaces. Research demonstrates that victims of cyberbullying frequently suffer from anxiety, low self-esteem, and academic decline. Protecting students from such harm is a fundamental manifestation of *Cinta Diri dan Sesama* (Love for Self and Others), as every individual possesses an inherent right to dignity, psychological safety, and mental peace.

Furthermore, a culture of digital empathy fosters a collaborative and supportive learning environment. When students practice compassionate communication online—thinking before commenting and supporting peers who face online hate—they create a safe space for everyone. Digital empathy teaches young people to view others not as online avatars, but as brothers and sisters in humanity (*Ukhuwah Insaniyah*). A school environment built on mutual love and respect empowers students to thrive academically and socially.

In conclusion, cyberbullying is a critical issue that demands immediate and decisive intervention. Therefore, schools **should** implement comprehensive digital ethics workshops, students **must** actively stand up against online harassment, and social media users **ought to** practice mindful, compassionate messaging. By embracing digital empathy, we fulfill our moral duty to protect one another and build a compassionate digital society.

### **Comprehension Questions**

1. **Main Idea**: What is the writer's main thesis in the text above?
2. **Detailed Information**: According to Argument 1, how does cyberbullying differ from traditional face-to-face bullying?
3. **Textual Inference**: Why does the author link digital empathy with the concept of *Ukhuwah Insaniyah* (Brotherhood in Humanity)?
4. **Language Feature Analysis**: Identify the generic structure of the text by locating the **Thesis**, **Argument 1**, **Argument 2**, and **Recommendation** paragraphs!
5. **Action Recommendation & KBC Reflection**: What 3 specific actions does the author recommend in the conclusion, and how do they reflect the principle of *Cinta Diri & Sesama*?

**Assessment**: Students must correctly answer 4 out of 5 questions demonstrating deep textual analysis and critical thinking skills.

## **E. WRITING**

**Target**: Construct persuasive sentences using modal auxiliaries and abstract nouns, and write a hortatory recommendation paragraph.

### **Writing Activity 1: Modal Auxiliaries Sentence Building**

Re-write and complete the following sentence starters to form strong hortatory recommendations:

1. (*Recommendation on mental health*) Students **should** ______________________________________________________.
2. (*Recommendation on environmental protection*) Local communities **must** ______________________________________.
3. (*Recommendation on digital ethics*) Social media users **ought to** ___________________________________________.
4. (*Warning on plastic pollution*) We **had better** stop dumping plastic in rivers, or else ___________________________.

### **Writing Activity 2: Drafting Thesis & Recommendation Paragraph**

Choose ONE of the following prompts and write a 100-120 word Hortatory Exposition paragraph focusing on the **Thesis** and **Recommendation** sections:

- **Prompt A**: Why teenagers should limit daily screen time to preserve mental health and real-life relationships.
- **Prompt B**: Why our madrasah must implement a daily "Zero Plastic Single-Use Policy".

**Writing Guidelines:**
- State a clear Thesis Statement in the first sentence.
- Include 1 strong supporting reason with an abstract noun (*awareness*, *responsibility*, *health*).
- Provide a strong Recommendation using *should*, *must*, or *ought to*.
- Incorporate a KBC reflection sentence (Cinta Diri/Sesama or Cinta Lingkungan).

**Assessment**: Evaluated on grammatical accuracy, coherence, effective use of modal auxiliaries, and alignment with KBC values.
