## 1. Vocabulary Section

**Objective:** To master advanced verbs and adverbs used in instructional texts related to mental health and personal development.

Study the following advanced instructional words:
- **Prioritize**: designate or treat something as more important than other things (Memprioritaskan)
- **Acknowledge**: accept or admit the existence or truth of (Mengakui/Menerima)
- **Declutter**: remove unnecessary items from an untidy or overcrowded place (Membersihkan/Merapikan)
- **Gradually**: in a gradual way; slowly; by degrees (Secara bertahap)
- **Mindfully**: in a conscious and aware manner (Dengan penuh kesadaran)

**Task**: Choose 3 of the words above. Write a short paragraph consisting of 3 imperative sentences giving advice to a friend who is feeling overwhelmed with schoolwork.
**Assessment**: Students must use at least 3 advanced words correctly to form cohesive, empathetic imperative sentences.

## 2. Listening Section

**Objective:** To analyze advanced instructional steps and infer the psychological benefits from an audio guide.

**Instructions:**
Listen to the audio (provided by the teacher) titled "5 Steps to Digital Detox". Answer the analytical questions below!

1. According to the audio, what is the first thing you should *acknowledge* before starting the detox?
2. What does the speaker mean by "declutter your digital space"?
3. Instead of scrolling through social media before bed, what alternative activity is suggested?
4. How does limiting screen time *gradually* help improve your mental health (Cinta Diri)?
5. Fill in the blank: "Always remember to consume information ___________, not compulsively."

**Assessment**: Students must answer at least 4 out of 5 questions correctly, demonstrating the ability to extract abstract instructions and infer benefits.

## 3. Speaking Section

**Objective:** To fluently present a set of tips/procedures using persuasive and empathetic intonation (Cinta Sesama).

**Instructions:**
In pairs, choose one of the following topics: 
- How to Overcome Academic Anxiety
- How to Build Self-Confidence
- How to Be a Good Listener to a Friend

Prepare a 2-minute "Tips & Tricks" presentation. 
- State your **Goal**.
- Present 3 **Steps** using advanced imperative verbs and sequence words.
- Maintain an encouraging and empathetic tone.

**Task**: Present your tips to the class.
**Assessment**: Evaluated on advanced pronunciation, fluency, persuasive/empathetic tone, and the accurate use of imperative structures.

## 4. Reading Section

**Objective:** To conduct a detailed comprehension and evaluation of an advanced procedure text (Tips).

**Instructions:**
Read the advanced text below.

**How to Practice Mindfulness in Daily Life**
Mindfulness is the practice of being fully present and engaged in the current moment. It is a profound act of *Cinta Diri* (Self-Love). If you feel stressed or disconnected, follow these steps to integrate mindfulness into your routine.
**First**, start your morning by breathing *mindfully*. Before checking your phone, sit quietly for two minutes and focus solely on your breath.
**Second**, *declutter* your workspace. A tidy environment reduces visual distractions and promotes a serene mind.
**Next**, *prioritize* single-tasking. Rather than juggling multiple assignments, focus completely on one task at a time.
**Finally**, *acknowledge* your emotions without judgment. If you feel anxious, simply label the feeling and let it pass, rather than fighting it.

**Questions:**
1. What is the definition of mindfulness according to the text?
2. Why is decluttering the workspace recommended?
3. What is the opposite of "single-tasking" mentioned in the text?
4. How should you handle negative emotions like anxiety based on the final step?
5. Do you think these steps are effective for high school students? Evaluate and give your reason!

**Assessment**: Students must provide analytical answers, successfully identifying instructions and critically evaluating their effectiveness.

## 5. Writing Section

**Objective:** To write a sophisticated, infographic-style procedure text (Tips) integrating KBC values.

**Instructions:**
Create the text for an Infographic titled: **"How to Be a Compassionate Friend (Cinta Sesama)"**.
- Write a short **Introductory sentence** (The Goal).
- Write **4-5 bulleted Steps**. Each step must start with a strong Imperative Verb (e.g., *Listen, Support, Avoid*).
- Include a brief explanation (1-2 sentences) for each step to elaborate *why* it is important.

**Assessment**: Evaluated on grammatical complexity (Imperative Verbs), structural clarity suitable for an infographic, and the depth of empathetic content.
