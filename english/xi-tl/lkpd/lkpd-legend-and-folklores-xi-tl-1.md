Long ago, on the beautiful island of Lombok, there lived a wise and beautiful princess named Mandalika. She was loved by all her people because of her (1) _______________, humble heart, and deep respect for nature and her community. When Princess Mandalika grew into a young woman, many princes from neighboring kingdoms wanted to marry her. Each prince threatened to start a (2) _______________ if she rejected his proposal.

Princess Mandalika was deeply troubled. She loved her people and could not bear to see her beloved homeland (3) _______________ by war and conflict. She spent days in silent reflection, seeking wisdom and praying to Almighty God for (4) _______________.

Finally, Princess Mandalika gathered all the princes and her people at Seger Beach at dawn. She stood on a high rock overlooking the ocean and spoke with words of deep (5) _______________: "My dear people and noble princes, I love all of you equally. I cannot choose one prince if it brings (6) _______________ and war to my people. Therefore, I dedicate my life to the peace and (7) _______________ of Lombok."

With those words, Princess Mandalika threw herself into the rolling waves of the ocean. The people rushed to save her, but she had (8) _______________ into the sparkling sea. In her place, millions of colorful sea worms, known as Nyale, appeared along the shoreline.

To this day, the people of Lombok celebrate the Bau Nyale festival, remembering Princess Mandalika's noble sacrifice, her selfless love for her (9) _______________, and her commitment to peace over (10) _______________.

#### Activity 1.2: Listening Comprehension Questions
Jawablah pertanyaan-pertanyaan berikut berdasarkan isi rekaman audio!

2. What dilemma did Princess Mandalika face when multiple princes proposed to her?
3. What spiritual and emotional action did Princess Mandalika take before making her final decision?
4. What transformed on Seger Beach after Princess Mandalika disappeared into the sea?
5. **KBC Reflection**: How does Princess Mandalika's decision reflect the values of *Cinta Tanah Air* (Love for Homeland) and *Cinta Diri & Sesama* (Love for Self & Others)?

### 3. Speaking Section

#### Dialogue Activity: Discussing Moral Lessons of Local and Global Legends
Praktikkan percakapan berpasangan (Pair Work) berikut dengan teman sekelasmu! Perhatikan pelafalan (*pronunciation*), kelancaran (*fluency*), serta intonasi empati (*Compassionate Communication*).

**Dialogue Script:**

**Aisyah** : "Hi Rahmad! Have you finished reading the text about Princess Mandalika and King Arthur?"  
**Rahmad** : "Hi Aisyah! Yes, I have. I am really moved by Princess Mandalika’s story. Her selfless love for her homeland is truly inspiring."  
**Aisyah** : "I agree with you! Instead of letting her ego take over, she chose unity and peace for her people. That is a clear example of *Cinta Tanah Air*."  
**Rahmad** : "Exactly. Compared to King Arthur who fought bravely with his sword Excalibur for justice, Mandalika fought with compassion and sacrifice."  
**Aisyah** : "Both legends show us that noble leaders always prioritize the well-being of others over their own desires."  
**Rahmad** : "That’s right. Learning these stories reminds me to practice kindness and avoid unnecessary conflicts in our daily school life."  
**Aisyah** : "Well said, Rahmad! Let’s share this positive lesson with our group."

#### Speaking Task:
1. Praktikkan dialog di atas di depan pasangan kelompokmu!
2. Buatlah 3 kalimat respon lisanmu sendiri yang menyatakan pendapatmu tentang tokoh legenda favoritmu di Indonesia!

**Assessment Target**: Kelancaran berbicara (fluency), pengucapan kata yang tepat (pronunciation), kontak mata, serta ekspresi yang mencerminkan rasa empati dan kesantunan (Compassionate Communication).

### 4. Reading Section

Bacalah teks naratif legenda berikut dengan saksama!

#### Text: The Legend of Batu Menangis (The Crying Stone)

Once upon a time, in a remote village in West Kalimantan, there lived a poor widow and her daughter. The daughter was exceptionally beautiful, but she was extraordinarily lazy and arrogant. She never helped her mother with housework and spent all her time admiring her own reflection in the mirror. Furthermore, she always demanded expensive clothes despite their poverty.

One sunny morning, the mother asked her daughter to accompany her to the market. The daughter agreed on one condition: her mother had to walk several steps behind her. She felt ashamed of her mother’s worn-out clothes and weathered skin. 

As they walked through the village market, a young man approached the daughter and asked, "Is this old woman walking behind you your mother?" The daughter turned her head proudly and replied, "Of course not! She is my servant."

Hearing those cruel words, the mother’s heart broke into pieces. She stopped walking, knelt on the dusty ground, and wept in silent agony. She raised her hands to heaven and prayed, "O Almighty God, forgive my weakness. But I cannot bear this humiliation any longer. Please teach my daughter a lesson so she may realize the value of a mother’s love."

Suddenly, the sky turned dark, and heavy thunder roared. A mysterious blue light encircled the daughter's feet. Slowly, her feet began to turn into hard gray stone. The stone crept upward to her knees, waist, and chest. Realizing her terrible sin, the daughter panicked and cried out in tears, "Oh mother, please forgive me! I am so sorry! Please pray for me!"

However, the consequences of her arrogant actions were irreversible. Her entire body turned completely into stone. Yet, tears continued to flow from her stone eyes. To this day, the rock is known as *Batu Menangis* (The Crying Stone), serving as a timeless reminder of the sacred bond between parents and children.

#### Reading Comprehension Questions:
1. **Main Idea**: What is the main theme of the story "The Legend of Batu Menangis"?
2. **Detail Extraction**: Why did the daughter demand that her mother walk behind her on their way to the market?
3. **Grammar & Features Analysis**: Identify two sentences in the text that use Simple Past Tense and one sentence that uses Direct Speech!
4. **Inference**: What can you infer about the mother's character before she prayed to God? Why did she refrain from shouting at her daughter in anger?
5. **KBC Character Reflection**: How does this story highlight the importance of *Cinta Diri & Sesama* (specifically respect for parents) and the dangers of arrogance (*ananiah*)?

**Assessment Target**: Peserta didik mampu menjawab ke-5 pertanyaan pemahaman bacaan dengan analisis kritis, logika bahasa yang tepat, serta kesadaran moral KBC (Skor minimal 80/100).
