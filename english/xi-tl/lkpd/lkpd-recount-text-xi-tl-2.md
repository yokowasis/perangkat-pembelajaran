## 1. Vocabulary Section

**Objective:** To master advanced action verbs and historical terminology, fostering a deeper appreciation for national history (Cinta Tanah Air).

Study the following advanced words used to recount historical events.
- **Proclaimed**: announced officially or publicly (Mendeklarasikan/Memproklamasikan)
- **Sacrificed**: surrendered or destroyed something for a higher purpose (Mengorbankan)
- **Defended**: protected from harm or danger (Mempertahankan/Membela)
- **Unyielding**: not giving way to pressure; resolute (Pangtang menyerah)
- **Commemorated**: remembered and shown respect for someone or something (Diperingati)

**Task**: Choose 3 of the words above. Write a short paragraph recounting a heroic act or a moment of struggle in Indonesian history.
**Assessment**: Students must use at least 3 advanced words correctly within a cohesive past tense paragraph.

## 2. Listening Section

**Objective:** To analyze specific details and historical context from an advanced recount audio track.

**Instructions:**
Listen to the audio (provided by the teacher) titled "The Battle of Surabaya". Answer the analytical questions below!

1. When was the independence of Indonesia *proclaimed*?
2. What triggered the massive conflict in Surabaya in November 1945?
3. How did the local heroes demonstrate their *unyielding* spirit against the allied forces?
4. Mention two specific sacrifices the people made during the battle!
5. Fill in the blank: "Every 10th of November is ___________ as Heroes' Day to honor their love for the nation."

**Assessment**: Students must answer at least 4 out of 5 questions correctly, demonstrating the ability to extract complex historical details.

---

## 3. Speaking Section

**Objective:** To fluently present a historical recount using persuasive intonation, inspiring a sense of patriotism (Cinta Tanah Air).

**Instructions:**
In pairs, pretend you are radio broadcasters in 1945 reporting the moments right after the Proclamation of Independence. 
- Use the Simple Past Tense to recount the events of August 17, 1945.
- Express the joy, relief, and the responsibility to *defend* the newly born nation.

**Task**: Present your radio broadcast to the class (2 minutes).
**Assessment**: Evaluated on advanced pronunciation, historical accuracy, expressive intonation, and emotional engagement with the audience.

## 4. Reading Section

**Objective:** To conduct a detailed comprehension and textual analysis of a historical recount.

**Instructions:**
Read the advanced text below.

**The Bandung Sea of Fire**
On March 24, 1946, a monumental event known as the Bandung Sea of Fire occurred. It was not merely a military tactic, but a profound demonstration of the people's *unyielding* love for their homeland. When the Allied forces demanded the Indonesian troops to vacate the city, the local fighters made a heartbreaking decision. Rather than surrendering their beloved city intact to the enemies, they deliberately burned down the southern part of Bandung. As they evacuated to the mountains, they looked back at the roaring flames. They *sacrificed* their homes and properties to *defend* the sovereignty of Indonesia. This tragic yet heroic event is forever *commemorated* in the song "Halo-Halo Bandung".

**Questions:**
1. Why did the event come to be known as the "Bandung Sea of Fire"?
2. What was the *unyielding* decision made by the local fighters?
3. How does the text portray the concept of 'Cinta Tanah Air' (Love for the Homeland)?
4. Identify two temporal connectives or time markers used in the text to show the sequence of events.
5. In your opinion, was burning the city a necessary sacrifice? Explain your reasoning!

**Assessment**: Students must provide analytical answers, identifying the text structure and deeply reflecting on the historical sacrifices made.

## 5. Writing Section

**Objective:** To write a sophisticated, multi-paragraph historical recount using complex sentences and temporal connectives.

**Instructions:**
Write a historical recount essay (3 paragraphs) about a national hero or a significant historical event in Indonesia.
- **Paragraph 1 (Orientation)**: Introduce the historical figure or event (Who, where, when).
- **Paragraph 2 (Events)**: Detail the sequence of events using the Simple Past Tense and clear temporal connectives (e.g., *subsequently, meanwhile, eventually*).
- **Paragraph 3 (Re-orientation)**: Conclude with a reflection on how their actions inspire our generation today (integrating *Cinta Tanah Air* and *Cinta Sesama*).

**Assessment**: Evaluated on grammatical complexity (Simple Past Tense), coherent chronological flow, and the depth of reflection in the concluding paragraph.
