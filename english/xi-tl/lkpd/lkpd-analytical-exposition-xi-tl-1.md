## **D. READING**

**Target**: Analyze the text structure, language features, and implicit moral values in an authentic analytical exposition essay.

### **Reading Text**

**Why Preserving Coastal Ecosystems in Bima is an Urgent Moral Duty**

Kota Bima and its surrounding coastal areas possess remarkable marine biodiversity and natural beauty. However, in recent years, the accumulation of plastic waste and improper trash disposal along the beaches of Teluk Bima have reached alarming levels. As responsible citizens and Muslim youth, we must recognize that protecting our coastal ecosystems is not merely an optional civic duty, but an urgent moral and ecological obligation (*Cinta Lingkungan*).

First and foremost, marine pollution severely threatens local biodiversity and marine life. Every year, thousands of marine creatures ingest microplastics or become entangled in abandoned fishing nets. Furthermore, plastic debris does not decompose easily; instead, it breaks down into toxic particles that enter the marine food chain, ultimately compromising human health when local communities consume contaminated seafood.

Secondly, coastal degradation directly damages Bima's local economy and tourism potential. Clean beaches attract visitors, support local fishermen, and sustain small coastal businesses. When our shorelines are littered with waste, tourists turn away, and fishing communities suffer economic hardship. Consequently, failing to maintain clean waters perpetuates poverty and environmental neglect.

From an ecotheological perspective (*Cinta Allah & Cinta Lingkungan*), humans are appointed as *khalifah* (stewards) on Earth. Islam strictly prohibits causing corruption (*fasad*) on land and sea. Caring for nature is a direct expression of gratitude for Allah's blessings.

In conclusion, coastal conservation in Bima is crucial for ecological preservation, economic stability, and spiritual integrity. Therefore, it is imperative that students, community members, and local leaders unite in implementing sustainable waste management and daily beach clean-up initiatives.
