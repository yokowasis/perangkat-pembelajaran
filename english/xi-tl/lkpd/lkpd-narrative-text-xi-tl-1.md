## 1. Vocabulary Section

**Objective:** To learn descriptive adjectives related to character traits and environmental settings in fables.

Read the following words carefully. They describe the traits of animals in fables.
- **Arrogant**: having an exaggerated sense of one's own importance (Sombong)
- **Humble**: having or showing a modest or low estimate of one's own importance (Rendah hati)
- **Generous**: showing a readiness to give more of something, such as money or time (Dermawan)
- **Greedy**: having an intense and selfish desire for something, especially wealth or food (Serakah)
- **Forgiving**: ready and willing to forgive (Pemaaf)

**Task**: Write 5 sentences using the Past Tense and the words above to describe animal characters in your favorite fables (e.g., *The arrogant lion, the greedy monkey*).
**Assessment**: Students must correctly use at least 4 out of 5 words in meaningful past tense sentences.

## 2. Listening Section

**Objective:** To extract the main conflict and resolution from an audio fable.

**Instructions:**
Listen to the audio (provided by the teacher) about "The Ant and The Grasshopper". Fill in the blanks below!

1. During the summer, the ant worked hard to gather ___________ for the winter.
2. Meanwhile, the ___________ grasshopper only played his violin and sang.
3. When winter came, the grasshopper was freezing and ___________.
4. The grasshopper knocked on the ant's door and begged for ___________.
5. The ant was ___________ and shared his food with the grasshopper.

**Assessment**: Students must answer at least 4 blanks correctly based on the listening track to demonstrate accurate information extraction.

## 3. Speaking Section

**Objective:** To practice fluency and pronunciation by role-playing a dialogue from a fable, focusing on expressive emotion.

**Instructions:**
Work with a partner. Practice the following dialogue between the Lion and the Mouse with correct pronunciation and intonation.

**Lion**: (Angry tone) "How dare you wake me up, little mouse! I will eat you!"
**Mouse**: (Scared tone) "Please spare me, O King of the Jungle. If you let me go, I will help you one day."
**Lion**: (Laughing arrogant tone) "Ha! How can a tiny mouse help a mighty lion? But I am full today, so I will let you go."
*(Days later, the lion is trapped in a net)*
**Mouse**: (Determined tone) "Don't worry, my friend. I will bite through these ropes and free you."

**Task**: Perform the dialogue in front of the teacher.
**Assessment**: Pronunciation clarity, fluency, and the ability to convey the emotional tone (arrogance vs. humility/compassion) will be evaluated.

## 4. Reading Section

**Objective:** To comprehend the structure (Orientation, Complication, Resolution) and moral lesson (Coda) of a fable.

**Instructions:**
Read the text below and answer the questions.

**The Greedy Dog**
Once upon a time, a dog was walking home with a delicious piece of meat in his mouth. As he crossed a small bridge over a clear stream, he looked down and saw his own reflection in the water. Thinking it was another dog with a larger piece of meat, he became greedy. He opened his mouth to bark at the "other dog" to steal its meat. However, the moment he opened his mouth, his own piece of meat fell into the stream and was washed away. The dog went home hungry and sad because of his own greed.

**Questions:**
1. What is the orientation (who, where) of the story?
2. What complication (problem) did the dog face on the bridge?
3. How did the story resolve (Resolution)?
4. What is the moral lesson (Coda) of the story relating to 'Cinta Sesama' or contentment?
5. Identify two Past Tense action verbs in the text!

**Assessment**: Students must be able to identify the narrative structure and accurately state the moral lesson.

---

## 5. Writing Section

**Objective:** To write a short narrative paragraph summarizing the resolution and coda of a story.

**Instructions:**
Imagine if the Grasshopper from the listening section met the Ant again the following summer. 
- Write a short paragraph (5-7 sentences) using Past Tense.
- Describe what the Grasshopper did differently this time (e.g., helping the ant gather food instead of playing).
- Conclude with a sentence about the importance of hard work and friendship.

**Assessment**: Grammar accuracy (Simple Past Tense), coherent storyline, and the clear presence of a moral conclusion will be assessed.
