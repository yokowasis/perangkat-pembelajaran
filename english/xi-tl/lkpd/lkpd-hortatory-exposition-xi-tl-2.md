## **D. READING**

**Target**: Analyze a full-length hortatory exposition text on civic duty, identify thesis, arguments, and recommendations, and evaluate transition markers.

### **Reading Text**

**Why Youth Must Protect Local Culture and Environmental Integrity in Bima**

Bima, located in West Nusa Tenggara, is a region rich in natural beauty, agricultural potential, and historical heritage. From the majestic Mount Tambora to the traditional *Uma Lengge* architecture and the vibrant woven cloth of *Tembe Nggoli*, Bima possesses an irreplaceable identity. However, modern environmental degradation and cultural apathy threaten to erode these treasures. Therefore, the youth of Bima must take proactive responsibility to safeguard our local environment and preserve our cultural heritage.

First and foremost, environmental degradation directly jeopardizes local livelihoods and food security. Widespread deforestation on hilly terrains for illegal agriculture has caused severe erosion and seasonal drought in several sub-districts. When hills are stripped bare, soil cannot absorb rainwater, leading to flash floods during monsoon seasons and water scarcity during dry months. Preserving our forests and rivers is not merely an ecological necessity; it is a fundamental act of *Cinta Lingkungan* (Love for Environment) and a religious mandate to avoid creating ruin on Earth (*fasad*).

Furthermore, protecting our cultural heritage strengthens our social cohesion and national identity (*Cinta Tanah Air*). In an era of rapid globalization, young people often overlook local traditions. Preserving Bima's historical sites, traditional arts, and indigenous wisdom regarding natural conservation builds civic pride and unity (*Ukhuwah Wathaniyah*). When youth honor their roots, they develop a resilient identity capable of standing strong against negative external influences.

In conclusion, passive concern is no longer sufficient. Therefore, young people **must** actively participate in local reforestation projects, community groups **should** organize cultural preservation workshops, and local government **ought to** enforce strict environmental protection laws. By taking action today, we fulfill our duty as patriotic citizens and pass on a prosperous, beautiful Bima to future generations.

### **Comprehension Questions**

1. **Main Idea**: What primary thesis does the author state regarding Bima youth?
2. **Detailed Information**: What specific environmental problems are caused by deforestation on local hills in Bima?
3. **Textual Inference**: How does preserving local culture and traditions contribute to *Ukhuwah Wathaniyah* (National Brotherhood)?
4. **Transition Marker Analysis**: Identify four transitional words/phrases used in the text to connect arguments (*First and foremost*, *Furthermore*, *In conclusion*, etc.) and explain their functions.
5. **Recommendation & KBC Synthesis**: What three recommendations are presented in the conclusion? How do these recommendations reflect the spirit of *Hubbul Wathan minal Iman* (Love for Homeland is part of Faith)?

**Assessment**: Students must answer 4 out of 5 questions correctly, demonstrating critical reading and synthesis skills.

## **E. WRITING**

**Target**: Write a complete 150-200 word Hortatory Exposition text (Letter to the Editor or Campaign Speech Script) recommending community action.

### **Writing Task**

Write a **Letter to the Editor** or a **Persuasive Campaign Speech** addressing a real issue in your school or city (MAN 1 Kota Bima / Bima City).

**Topic Options:**
- **Option 1**: Recommending better waste management and recycling facilities in school.
- **Option 2**: Recommending youth campaigns against digital hoaxes and bullying.
- **Option 3**: Recommending student-led tree planting along Bima's coastal areas.

**Required Text Layout:**

```text
[Title / Salutation]

[Paragraph 1: THESIS STATEMENT]
- Introduce the issue.
- State your clear opinion and why action is urgent.

[Paragraph 2 & 3: SUPPORTING ARGUMENTS]
- Provide 2 distinct arguments using transition words (Furthermore, Consequently).
- Include abstract nouns and facts/reflections.
- Connect with KBC values (Cinta Lingkungan / Cinta Tanah Air / Cinta Diri & Sesama).

[Paragraph 4: RECOMMENDATION]
- Use modal auxiliaries (should, must, ought to, had better).
- State 2-3 concrete actions for students, school, or government.
- Closing inspiring call to action.
```

**Writing Guidelines:**
- Word count: 150 - 200 words.
- Grammar: Use Simple Present Tense, Modal Auxiliaries, Action Verbs, and Abstract Nouns.
- Ensure correct spelling, punctuation, and paragraphing.

**Assessment**: Evaluated using the standard writing rubric criteria (Content, Organization, Vocabulary, Language Use, Mechanics, KBC Alignment).
