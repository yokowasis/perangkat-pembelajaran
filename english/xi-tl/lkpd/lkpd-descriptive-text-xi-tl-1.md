## 1. Vocabulary Section

**Objective:** To learn new adjectives to describe people's character and the beauty of nature, appreciating God's creations.

Read the following adjectives carefully. They are used to describe positive traits and beautiful places.

- **Breathtaking**: extremely beautiful or amazing (Sangat indah/memukau)
- **Pristine**: in its original condition; unspoiled (Asri/alami)
- **Compassionate**: feeling or showing sympathy and concern for others (Penuh kasih sayang)
- **Courageous**: not deterred by danger or pain; brave (Pemberani)
- **Majestic**: having or showing impressive beauty or dignity (Megah)

**Task**: Write 5 sentences using the words above to describe your favorite person or place!
**Assessment**: Students must correctly use at least 4 out of 5 words in meaningful sentences to demonstrate vocabulary mastery.

## 2. Listening Section

**Objective:** To extract specific information from an audio describing a local hero or a natural wonder.

**Instructions:**
Listen to the audio (provided by the teacher) about "Raja Ampat, The Pristine Paradise". Fill in the blanks below!

[Raja Ampat](../../../docs/raja_ampat.mp3 ":include :type=audio")

1. Raja Ampat is located in ___________ Papua.
2. The word 'Ampat' means ___________ because there are four major islands.
3. It is famous for its ___________ marine life, a true blessing from God.
4. We must keep the ocean clean because throwing trash is an act of ___________ (destroying nature).
5. The local people are very ___________ to visitors.

**Assessment**: Students must answer at least 4 blanks correctly based on the listening track to demonstrate accurate information extraction.

## 3. Speaking Section

**Objective:** To practice fluency and pronunciation while discussing ways to preserve our environment (Cinta Lingkungan).

**Instructions:**
Work with a partner. Practice the following dialogue with correct pronunciation and intonation.

**A**: "Did you visit the new botanical garden yesterday?"
**B**: "Yes, I did! It was absolutely breathtaking. The flowers were so colorful."
**A**: "I heard they have a strict rule about littering."
**B**: "That's right. They want to keep it pristine. It's our duty to protect such a majestic place."
**A**: "I agree. Caring for nature is a way to show our gratitude to the Creator."

**Task**: Perform the dialogue in front of the teacher.
**Assessment**: Pronunciation clarity, fluency, and the ability to convey the emotional tone (compassion) will be evaluated.

## 4. Reading Section

**Objective:** To comprehend the main idea and details of a descriptive text about an inspiring figure (Cinta Sesama).

**Instructions:**
Read the text below and answer the questions.

**Ki Hajar Dewantara**
Ki Hajar Dewantara is a prominent figure in Indonesian education. He was a courageous and compassionate leader who dedicated his life to educating the nation. Physically, he was often seen wearing traditional Javanese attire and glasses, showing his humble personality. He believed that education should liberate people and build good character. His famous motto, "Tut Wuri Handayani" (from behind, a teacher must give encouragement), is still used today. He inspires us to love seeking knowledge (Cinta Ilmu) and to care for our society.

**Questions:**

1. What is the main idea of the text?
2. Mention two adjectives used to describe Ki Hajar Dewantara's character!
3. How did he show his humble personality physically?
4. What does "Tut Wuri Handayani" mean?
5. Why do you think he is considered a compassionate leader?

**Assessment**: Students must be able to identify the main idea and infer the character traits from the text accurately.

---

## 5. Writing Section

**Objective:** To produce a short descriptive text promoting a local hero or a beautiful place, integrating KBC values.

**Instructions:**
Think of a person you admire in your family/school, or a beautiful natural spot in your hometown. Write a short descriptive text (2 paragraphs).

- **Paragraph 1 (Identification)**: Introduce the person/place.
- **Paragraph 2 (Description)**: Describe their physical appearance, qualities, or the scenery. Include a sentence about why we should appreciate them (Cinta Diri dan Sesama or Cinta Lingkungan).

**Assessment**: Grammar accuracy (Simple Present Tense), vocabulary usage (Adjectives), and text structure (Identification & Description) will be assessed for coherence.
