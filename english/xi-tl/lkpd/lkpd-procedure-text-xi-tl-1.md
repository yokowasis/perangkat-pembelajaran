## 1. Vocabulary Section

**Objective:** To learn essential imperative verbs used in daily manuals and recipes.

Read the following action verbs carefully. They are used to give commands or instructions.
- **Chop**: cut something into small pieces with repeated sharp blows (Mencincang/Memotong)
- **Stir**: move a spoon or other implement around in a liquid (Mengaduk)
- **Unplug**: disconnect an electrical device by pulling out its plug (Mencabut kabel)
- **Insert**: place, fit, or thrust something into another thing (Memasukkan)
- **Recycle**: convert waste into reusable material (Mendaur ulang)

**Task**: Write 5 imperative sentences using the words above to instruct someone on how to make a healthy salad OR how to safely turn off an electronic device.
**Assessment**: Students must correctly use at least 4 out of 5 words to form accurate imperative sentences.

## 2. Listening Section

**Objective:** To extract specific chronological steps from a procedure audio.

**Instructions:**
Listen to the audio (provided by the teacher) about "How to Make an Eco-Friendly Plant Pot from a Plastic Bottle" (Cinta Lingkungan). Fill in the blanks below!

1. First, make sure you wash and ___________ the plastic bottle thoroughly.
2. Next, use a pair of scissors to ___________ the bottle in half.
3. Carefully ___________ some small holes at the bottom for water drainage.
4. After that, ___________ the bottom half with soil and plant your seed.
5. Finally, don't forget to ___________ your new plant every morning.

**Assessment**: Students must answer at least 4 blanks correctly based on the listening track to demonstrate accurate information extraction.

## 3. Speaking Section

**Objective:** To practice fluency and clear articulation while giving step-by-step instructions.

**Instructions:**
Work with a partner. Practice the following dialogue about how to connect a phone to a Bluetooth speaker. Pay attention to the sequence words!

**A**: "Can you show me how to connect this speaker to my phone?"
**B**: "Sure! **First**, turn on the speaker by pressing the power button."
**A**: "Okay, it's on. What's **next**?"
**B**: "**Then**, go to your phone's settings and turn on the Bluetooth."
**A**: "I see the device name. Should I click it?"
**B**: "Yes. **Finally**, wait for the beep sound, and you can start playing your music!"

**Task**: Perform the dialogue in front of the teacher.
**Assessment**: Pronunciation clarity, fluency, and the correct intonation of sequence words will be evaluated.

## 4. Reading Section

**Objective:** To comprehend the structure (Goal, Materials, Steps) and specific details of a procedure text.

**Instructions:**
Read the text below and answer the questions.

**How to Make a Simple Organic Fertilizer**
(Goal) Creating your own organic fertilizer is a great way to reduce waste and love our environment (Cinta Lingkungan). 
(Materials) You will need: leftover vegetable scraps, dried leaves, soil, water, and a large container with a lid.
(Steps)
1. **First**, collect all your vegetable scraps (like carrot peels or banana skins). Do not include meat or dairy.
2. **Second**, chop the scraps into smaller pieces so they decompose faster.
3. **Next**, put a layer of dried leaves at the bottom of the container.
4. **Then**, add the vegetable scraps and cover them with a layer of soil.
5. **After that**, sprinkle a little water to keep it moist, but not too wet.
6. **Finally**, close the lid tightly and wait for about 3-4 weeks until it turns into dark, nutrient-rich soil.

**Questions:**
1. What is the goal of the text?
2. Mention two materials needed to make the fertilizer!
3. Why do we need to chop the vegetable scraps into smaller pieces?
4. What should you NOT put into the container?
5. How long does it take for the mixture to become fertilizer?

**Assessment**: Students must be able to identify the procedural structure and extract specific instructional details accurately.

## 5. Writing Section

**Objective:** To produce a short procedure text using Imperative Sentences and Sequence Words.

**Instructions:**
Write a simple procedure text (1 page) about a daily task you are good at (e.g., How to cook your favorite instant noodles, How to make a glass of tea, or How to safely cross a busy street).
- Include the **Goal** as the title.
- List the **Materials/Tools** needed.
- Write at least 4 **Steps** using sequence words (*First, Next, Then, Finally*) and imperative verbs.

**Assessment**: Grammar accuracy (Imperative Sentences), proper use of sequence words, and structural completeness will be assessed.
