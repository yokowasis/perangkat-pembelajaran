Part One: Personal Experience and Gratitude.

Listen carefully to the personal recount.

Last year, during the rainy season, my friends and I participated in a youth community service project in our village near Kota Bima. Early in the morning at 6:00 AM, we gathered at the village office with shovels, saplings, and cleaning tools.

First, we cleaned the drainage channels to prevent localized flooding that often affected lower neighborhood areas.

Then, we planted fifty avocado and mango trees around the community hall. Many elderly residents came out to cheer us on and brought homemade tea and traditional snacks.

After working for four hours under the humid weather, we felt completely exhausted. However, seeing the clean environment and the happy smiles on our neighbors' faces filled our hearts with immense gratitude and warmth.

Finally, we gathered for a short reflection session where our group leader reminded us that helping others and caring for our environment is a true expression of gratitude to God. It was an unforgettable day that strengthened our bond of friendship and self-compassion.

Part Two: Historical Hero Recount.

Listen to the historical recount about Sultan Salahuddin of Bima.

Sultan Muhammad Salahuddin was one of the most respected figures in the history of Bima, West Nusa Tenggara. He ruled the Sultanate of Bima from 1915 until his passing in 1951.

First, during his leadership, Sultan Salahuddin focused heavily on education and Islamic values. He established modern Islamic schools and encouraged young people in Bima to pursue knowledge diligently.

Second, when the struggle for Indonesian independence intensified after 1945, Sultan Salahuddin showed strong national leadership. He officially declared that the Sultanate of Bima joined and supported the newly born Republic of Indonesia.

Third, he mobilised local resources and supported freedom fighters against colonial rule, demonstrating deep love for the homeland, known as Hubbul Wathan minal Iman.

Finally, his dedication to social justice, education, and unity left a lasting legacy for the people of Bima and Indonesia.
