Good morning, students. Today I would like to share an important analytical argument regarding digital literacy and youth mental health. In this modern era, social media has become an inseparable part of teenagers' daily lives. However, I strongly believe that excessive screen time and uncritical social media consumption severely harm adolescent mental wellbeing. Firstly, spending too many hours scrolling through social media platforms increases anxiety and feelings of inadequacy. Teenagers often compare their real lives with curated, unnatural online photos. Secondly, online spaces can foster negative behaviors like cyberbullying if we lack empathy and self-discipline. According to recent psychological studies, students who practice digital detox and mindful screen habits report higher self-esteem, better sleep quality, and stronger interpersonal relationships. Therefore, it is imperative for young people to cultivate self-love and compassion towards others when navigating the digital world. Protecting our minds from toxic digital habits is not just a personal choice, but a moral responsibility.
