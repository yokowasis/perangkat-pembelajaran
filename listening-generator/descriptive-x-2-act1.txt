[Listening Activity 2: Sambori Traditional Village]
Listen to the description of Sambori Village in Bima and answer the questions on your worksheet.
Sambori Village is a charming traditional village situated in the highlands of Bima, West Nusa Tenggara. Located high in the mountains, the village has fresh, cool air and breathtaking panoramic views. The traditional wooden houses, known as Uma Lengge, have high thatched roofs and high wooden stilts. The villagers are exceptionally hospitable, polite, and proud of their cultural heritage. They preserve their surrounding forests with deep respect, avoiding any destruction to nature. Visiting Sambori Village inspires us to cherish our local culture and preserve God's natural creation.
