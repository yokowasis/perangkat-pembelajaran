[Listening Activity 3: Komodo National Park]
Listen to the description of Komodo National Park and choose True or False for each statement.
Komodo National Park is a famous national park located in East Nusa Tenggara, Indonesia. It consists of three major islands: Komodo, Rinca, and Padar. The park is famous as the natural habitat of the Komodo dragon, the world's largest living lizard. The landscapes are truly magnificent, featuring rugged hills, pristine beaches, and turquoise ocean water. Visitors are reminded to protect the environment and avoid damaging nature. Preserving this national heritage is an expression of our love for our country and environment.

Now, answer the true or false questions on your worksheet.
Number 1: Komodo National Park consists of three main islands.
Number 2: The Komodo dragon is the smallest lizard in the world.
Number 3: Visitors are allowed to damage nature in the national park.
Number 4: The park has rugged hills and turquoise water.
Number 5: Preserving national heritage reflects love for our country.
