Welcome to the English Listening Practice for Grade Ten Unit Two Recount Text Part Two.

Part One: School Eco-Project Recount.

Listen carefully to the narrative about MAN 1 Kota Bima's environmental campaign.

Last month, students of MAN 1 Kota Bima conducted a coastal cleanup and mangrove planting project at Lawata Beach.

First, on Friday afternoon, three hundred students and twenty teachers arrived at Lawata Beach wearing green uniforms and protective gloves.

Second, we divided into four groups. Group One collected plastic bottles and trash from the shoreline, while Group Two planted two hundred young mangrove seedlings along the coastal wetland.

Third, Group Three set up educational signs reminding visitors to keep the beach clean and refrain from harming coastal vegetation.

After three hours of hard work, we collected over fifteen sacks of plastic waste.

Finally, we held a closing circle near the beach. Our school principal praised our dedication to protecting marine life and preserving nature as an act of faith and Cinta Lingkungan. We returned home exhausted but proud of our contribution.

Part Two: Digital Diary and Vlog Script Recount.

Listen to a student's digital diary entry about creating a video vlog.

Hi everyone! Today I want to share my experience of creating a digital vlog script for our English assignment.

First, my project partner Aisyah and I selected the topic of digital ethics and respectful communication among teenagers.

Second, last Tuesday, we wrote a detailed script outlining our personal experiences with social media and how we learned to practice self-compassion and avoid negative online behavior.

Third, on Wednesday, we filmed our presentation in the school library using a smartphone and a portable microphone. We rehearsed three times to ensure clear pronunciation and natural intonation.

Finally, after editing the video with subtitles and background music, we uploaded our vlog script presentation to our class digital folder. It was a challenging yet rewarding experience that improved both our English speaking skills and digital literacy.
