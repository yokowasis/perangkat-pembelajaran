Welcome to the Grade 11 English Listening Activity for Unit 3: Hortatory Exposition.

Please listen carefully to the following talk about promoting kindness and digital empathy in schools. You will use this audio to complete Listening Activity 1 and Listening Activity 2 in your worksheet.

"Good morning everyone. Today, I would like to address an important issue that affects many teenagers: online behavior and mental well-being. With the rise of social media, cyberbullying has become a serious problem. Therefore, schools and students should actively build a culture of digital empathy and mutual respect.

First of all, digital harassment damages a student's self-esteem and emotional health. When young people receive hurtful comments online, they often experience anxiety and isolation. Showing compassion to ourselves and others is essential for maintaining psychological well-being.

Secondly, a safe online environment encourages positive learning and collaboration. If students feel supported, they can express their ideas freely without fear of judgment. Kindness online creates a warm community where everyone feels valued.

In conclusion, we ought to take immediate action against cyberbullying. We should report harmful online behavior, support victims of bullying, and practice mindful communication on social media. Let us treat every person online with the same love, respect, and dignity that we wish to receive."

Now, please answer the questions in your LKPD worksheet. Thank you for listening.
