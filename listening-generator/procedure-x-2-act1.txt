Listening Activity 1: Listen and Sequence.
Listen to the guide on how to design an effective educational video tutorial script. Put the steps in the correct order from one to six as you hear them.

First, define your main goal and target audience clearly before writing any line.
Second, select a meaningful educational topic that embodies values of knowledge and love for learning.
Third, list all required materials, tools, or digital software needed for the procedure.
Fourth, write the spoken audio script alongside visual cues in a two-column format.
Fifth, review your script with a peer using compassionate communication to refine clarity and grammar.
Sixth, rehearse your spoken tutorial with proper pronunciation and enthusiastic body language.

Listening Activity 2: Listen and Complete.
Listen to the expert tips on recording video tutorials and fill in the missing words in your worksheet.

Recording an Impactful Video Tutorial:
First, set up your camera on a steady tripod to keep your visual framing stable.
Second, ensure your voice is clear by using a microphone and recording in a quiet room.
Third, use clear action verbs and sequence connectors like first, next, and after that.
Fourth, demonstrate each step slowly so viewers can follow along without getting confused.
Fifth, close your video tutorial with an encouraging reflection that inspires your audience.

When creating an educational tutorial video, introducing your topic with enthusiasm and sincerity creates an instant connection with your viewers.
