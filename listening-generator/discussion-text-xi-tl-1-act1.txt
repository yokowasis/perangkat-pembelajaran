Unit 4: Discussion Text. Part 1: Artificial Intelligence in Education.

Activity 1: Listen to the following discussion text and identify the main issue and key arguments.

Artificial intelligence tools, such as generative AI chatbots and automated writing assistants, have rapidly entered high school classrooms worldwide. This technological leap has sparked a major debate among educators, students, and parents regarding whether AI is a catalyst for modern learning or a serious threat to academic integrity.

On one hand, supporters argue that AI tools provide personalized learning support. Proponents point out that AI can explain complex scientific concepts, offer instant feedback on writing drafts, and assist students with diverse learning needs. Furthermore, evidence suggests that responsible AI usage fosters digital literacy, preparing students for an AI-driven future workforce where technology integration is essential.

On the other hand, opponents express strong concerns about intellectual dishonesty and the decline of critical thinking. Critics contend that over-reliance on AI generators may encourage plagiarism and reduce students' motivation to read, write, and analyze independently. In addition, it appears that automated tools cannot replace the human values of empathy, moral judgment, and genuine teacher-student interaction.

In conclusion, it could be argued that AI in education should neither be uncritically embraced nor entirely banned. A balanced approach, guided by strong ethics and intellectual integrity, ensures that technology serves as a tool for empowerment while preserving core human values.
