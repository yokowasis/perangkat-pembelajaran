Welcome to Grade X English Listening Exercise for Narrative Text Meeting 1 and 2.

Listening Activity 1: Listen to the fable about the Wise Deer and the Forest Stream.
Once upon a time, in a peaceful green forest, lived a gentle deer named Pip. Pip loved all creatures and cared for the clean river. One dry summer, a thirsty wolf arrived at the stream. Instead of fighting for water, Pip shared the cool stream and showed the wolf where fresh plants grew. The wolf was touched by Pip's kindness and promised never to harm the forest animals or destroy the river.

Listening Activity 2: Listen to the sentences and fill in the missing words.
Number 1. The gentle deer named Pip cared for the clean river.
Number 2. One dry summer, a thirsty wolf came to the forest stream.
Number 3. Pip shared the cool stream instead of fighting.
Number 4. The wolf was touched by Pip's kindness and empathy.
Number 5. Protecting nature and loving others brings peace to everyone.

Listening Activity 3: Listen to the statements and decide if they are True or False.
Number 1. Pip was a greedy animal who refused to share water.
Number 2. The wolf came to the forest during a cold rainy winter.
Number 3. Pip showed compassion and kindness to the thirsty wolf.
Number 4. The wolf promised to protect the animals and the forest stream.
Number 5. Showing love to nature and fellow creatures creates a peaceful community.
