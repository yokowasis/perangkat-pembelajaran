Honorable teacher and my beloved classmates. Today, I stand before you to advocate for a fundamental cause: why young Indonesian intellectuals must champion scientific literacy and academic integrity. In an era dominated by rapid technological advancement and information overload, our nation urgently needs youth who seek knowledge with passion and honesty. First and foremost, scientific literacy equips us to separate verified truth from misinformation and logical fallacies. As Muslim scholars guided by the principle of Cinta Ilmu, we have a religious and civic duty to base our arguments on empirical facts and authentic references. Furthermore, intellectual honesty and avoiding plagiarism reflect our moral maturity and respect for knowledge creators. When young people write and speak with evidence-based reasoning, they contribute meaningfully to community development and national progress. In conclusion, loving knowledge is not merely about achieving high grades; it is about building a strong, truth-seeking nation grounded in faith and wisdom. Let us dedicate ourselves to lifelong learning and ethical scholarship for the glory of Indonesia.
