Welcome to English Listening Practice for Grade Ten, Unit Four: Procedure Text Part One.

Listening Activity 1: Listen and Number.
Listen carefully to the steps for practicing water-saving wudhu, an eco-friendly thaharah habit. Number the steps from one to six in the correct order as you hear them.

First, turn on the water tap with a very small stream to avoid wasting water.
Second, wash both hands up to the wrists three times while maintaining a mindful heart.
Third, rinse your mouth and cleanse your nose gently using a minimal amount of water.
Fourth, wash your full face three times with intention and gratitude to Allah.
Fifth, wash your arms up to the elbows and wipe your head and ears carefully.
Sixth, wash both feet up to the ankles and turn off the water tap completely.

Listening Activity 2: Listen and Complete.
Listen to the tips for maintaining digital mental health and fill in the missing words in your worksheet.

How to Protect Your Digital Mental Health:
First, limit your daily screen time on social media platforms to avoid mental fatigue.
Second, turn off non-essential notifications so you can stay focused during your study time.
Third, unfollow accounts that trigger negative emotions, anxiety, or unhealthy comparison.
Fourth, practice compassionate communication by posting kind words and avoiding cyberbullying.
Fifth, take regular digital detox breaks, especially before going to sleep at night.

Listening Activity 3: Listen and Statement Review.
Listen to the following statements and decide whether they are true or false based on the previous tips.

Statement 1: Turning on the water tap with a full stream is recommended during wudhu.
Statement 2: Prophet Muhammad taught us to avoid wasting water even when performing ritual purification.
Statement 3: Keeping all social media notifications active helps improve study concentration.
Statement 4: Posting kind words on social media is a form of Cinta Diri and Sesama.
Statement 5: Taking a digital detox before sleeping promotes better mental health and restful sleep.
