Welcome to Grade X English Listening Exercise for Narrative Text Meeting 3 and 4.

Listening Activity 1: Listen to the story of the Dragon and the Peaceful Village.
In a faraway valley, a fierce dragon lived on a rocky mountain. The villagers were afraid because the dragon burned their crops when he was angry. But young Ayla noticed the dragon was acting in pain. She climbed the mountain with medicine and discovered a thorn trapped in the dragon's paw. Ayla gently removed the thorn with compassionate care. The dragon felt immense relief and gratitude. Instead of destroying the village, the dragon became its protector, helping to water the fields and live in harmony with the people.

Listening Activity 2: Listen to the sentences and complete the missing words.
Number 1. The villagers were afraid of the mountain dragon.
Number 2. Ayla noticed that the dragon was suffering from pain.
Number 3. Ayla gently pulled out a sharp thorn from the dragon's paw.
Number 4. The dragon felt deep gratitude for Ayla's compassionate help.
Number 5. True reconciliation restored peace and harmony between humans and nature.

Listening Activity 3: Listen to the statements and choose the correct answer.
Number 1. What was the main cause of the dragon's anger? The dragon had a sharp thorn in its paw.
Number 2. How did Ayla respond to the dragon? With compassion, care, and courage.
Number 3. What happened after the thorn was removed? The dragon became the peaceful protector of the village.
Number 4. What moral value is shown in the story? Peacemaking and empathy can transform conflict into reconciliation.
