Welcome to the Grade 11 English Listening Activity for Unit 3: Hortatory Exposition, Part 2.

Please listen carefully to the following speech about environmental protection and community action in Bima. You will use this audio to complete the listening exercises in your worksheet.

"Honorable teachers and my dear friends. I stand before you today to talk about our civic responsibility toward our natural environment. As residents of Bima, we are blessed with beautiful mountains, fertile lands, and coastal areas. However, plastic waste and deforestation are threatening our environment and our future. Therefore, local communities must take urgent collective action to preserve our ecosystems.

First, plastic pollution severely harms our rivers and marine life. Single-use plastic bottles and bags clog our drainage channels, causing local flooding during the rainy season. By reducing plastic consumption and recycling waste, we can protect our soil and water sources from toxic contamination.

Second, loving our environment is a reflection of our faith and patriotism. Preserving nature is not just a personal choice; it is a sacred duty to future generations and to our nation. A clean environment fosters a healthy society and strengthens our national resilience.

Therefore, I strongly recommend that our school and local neighborhood establish weekly clean-up programs, plant more native trees, and eliminate single-use plastics. Let us join hands today to safeguard our land with love, dedication, and active participation!"

Now, please complete the exercises in your LKPD worksheet. Thank you.
