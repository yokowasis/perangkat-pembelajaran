Activity 1: Listening to a Local Legend.
Listen carefully to the narrative text about The Legend of Princess Mandalika of Lombok.

Long ago, on the beautiful island of Lombok, there lived a wise and beautiful princess named Mandalika. She was loved by all her people because of her kindness, humble heart, and deep respect for nature and her community. When Princess Mandalika grew into a young woman, many princes from neighboring kingdoms wanted to marry her. Each prince threatened to start a war if she rejected his proposal.

Princess Mandalika was deeply troubled. She loved her people and could not bear to see her beloved homeland destroyed by war and conflict. She spent days in silent reflection, seeking wisdom and praying to Almighty God for guidance.

Finally, Princess Mandalika gathered all the princes and her people at Seger Beach at dawn. She stood on a high rock overlooking the ocean and spoke with words of deep compassion: "My dear people and noble princes, I love all of you equally. I cannot choose one prince if it brings suffering and war to my people. Therefore, I dedicate my life to the peace and unity of Lombok."

With those words, Princess Mandalika threw herself into the rolling waves of the ocean. The people rushed to save her, but she had vanished into the sparkling sea. In her place, millions of colorful sea worms, known as Nyale, appeared along the shoreline.

To this day, the people of Lombok celebrate the Bau Nyale festival, remembering Princess Mandalika's noble sacrifice, her selfless love for her homeland, and her commitment to peace over division.
