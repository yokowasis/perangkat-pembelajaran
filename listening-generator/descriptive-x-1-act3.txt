Listening Activity 3: True or False.
Listen to the description and decide if it is true or false.

Number 1. There is a small dog in front of the house. The dog is very cute.

Number 2. There is a red car next to the house. The car is clean and beautiful.

Number 3. A girl with long hair is standing near the tree. She is wearing a blue dress.

Number 4. The house is new, not old. It has two windows and a brown door.

Number 5. There is a tall tree beside the house. The tree has many green leaves.
