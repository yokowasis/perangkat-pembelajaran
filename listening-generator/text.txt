Raja Ampat, the Pristine Paradise.

Raja Ampat is one of the most beautiful places in Indonesia. It is located in West Papua. The word "Ampat" means four because there are four major islands: Waigeo, Misool, Salawati, and Batanta. Raja Ampat is famous for its incredible marine life, a true blessing from God. More than 75 percent of the world's coral species can be found here.

We must keep the ocean clean because throwing trash is an act of fasad, which means destroying nature. The local people are very welcoming to visitors. They believe that protecting nature is part of their duty as human beings. That is why Raja Ampat remains a pristine paradise for everyone to enjoy.
